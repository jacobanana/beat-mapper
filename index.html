Warp

The audio re-timed so every beat of the map lands on a straight grid at one tempo. The tempo map is made in Beats; here it is only read. The tempo lane shows the grid's tempo dashed, and how far each bar moves to reach it
Warped, in the top bar: what plays. Warped plays it with the click on the grid, and the waveform is drawn where the warp puts it. Slice and Groove follow the same switchW
From loop: loop a section that is played right, and the grid takes the tempo it averages, to the nearest BPM. Or type a tempo; empty takes the average of what is warpedF
Align: drag a transient in the upper half onto a grid line, and the warp puts that hit exactly on it, stretching the audio either side to fit. Double-tap a transient to put it on the nearest line, or a warp marker to let it go. Alt drops it off the grid
Quantize: every transient of what is warped moves that far towards the grid line nearest it (0% by default, as played). Moving it, the grid or the shuffle warps again, and what plays follows once you let go. Hear the difference with Warped (W). The only quantize: the slices, the pocket and the drum MIDI follow it
whole file · just the loopWhat is warped, heard and saved. Drawing a loop to take its tempo doesn't make it the loop alone
MaterialHow it is stretched: Drums cut at the transients, Mono and Vocal overlap-add, Poly a phase vocoder, Full mix both, Re-pitch like a turntable
Reset: the whole file at the tempo it averages, Full mix, no quantize, and no warp markers (undo brings them back)

Slice samples

Every transient from step 1 starts a slice, running to the next transient (plus a tail) or for a fixed length. Switch the loop on and only what is inside it is sliced. Heard warped, each slice is cut from the warped audio, so it is what you hear, on the grid
Transients closer together than this are skipped, so the slice before them keeps running
What each .wav gets: click-free fades, per-slice normalizing to the target dBFS, source channels or a mono mix, 16 or 24-bit, at the file's sample rate or another, dithered or not. Set in Export

Groove

Kick, snare and hats are found in the audio, and each hit is measured against the beat. The waveform shows them in three lanes, each with a tail back to its grid line. Map the beats first (step 2) if the take drifts
SourceDrums only, or a full mix (experimental: the other instruments are modelled apart from the drums, but expect misses)
K S HSensitivity per voice, with how many hits each has. Raise the snare's for ghost notes
Reset: the hits as found at the starting sensitivities, measured as they start
Hear the drums as MIDI on a synth kit: in the mixer, tap its icon to unmute it, and the audio's to hear the kit aloneM
ChartLeft: where each voice sits, ahead or behind – every hit, its middle half, and its median. Right: the typical bar – dot size is velocity, faint dots are steps played in only some bars, tails show the lean
Heard warped, the hits are measured, heard on the kit and exported where the warp puts them: quantized in the Warp step, they sit on the grid. Heard as the original, where they were playedW
Save the drums as MIDI (as heard: warped, at the grid's one tempo and lined up with the warped .wav; the original, on the tempo map), or the typical bar as a Pocket Science groove file: in ExportCtrl/⌘E
